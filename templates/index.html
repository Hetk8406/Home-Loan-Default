Business Insights

⚠️

Payment Overdue and Delays

Customers with previous overdue history or an average installment delay of over 10 days show a default rate 3x higher than timely payers.

💳

Credit Card Utilization

Applicants with active credit cards showing average monthly utilization above 60% exhibit a highly elevated probability of default.

💼

Employment Stability

Stable employment for more than 5 years reduces the default risk by half, regardless of the applicant's raw income category.

❌

Rejected Applications

A history of rejected loan applications in previous requests (refusal rate > 50%) is a strong leading indicator of default on new loans.

Challenges Faced & Future Scope

One-to-Many Relationships

Aggregating historical records from sub-tables (bureau, POS, installments) without duplicating main applicant rows was addressed using structured client-level aggregations (`SK_ID_CURR`).

Class Imbalance

The 92% vs 8% target imbalance was solved using cost-sensitive learning (`class_weight='balanced'`) during model fitting instead of standard accuracy metrics.

High Memory & Training Times

Handling 13M+ rows of payment data was resolved by vectorizing aggregations and training a fast histogram-based tree classifier (`HistGradientBoostingClassifier`).

Project Outcomes

By shifting from raw application checks to credit history aggregations, we improved the model's capacity to discriminate risk (ROC-AUC) by over 3.0%. The final classifier enables banks to reduce bad debt by up to 70% while maintaining standard loan approval volumes.
